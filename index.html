Our services

Everything we do, so you can find the right one. Not sure which you need? Just call.

Domestic electrical

Installation. New circuits, sockets and lighting fitted by a qualified electrician and tested before we leave.

Repairs and upgrades. Faults found and fixed, and older wiring or fuse boards brought up to date.

EICR solutions

Inspection. An electrical installation condition report (EICR) for homes and commercial premises.

Clear results. A written report that shows what is safe, what needs attention and what to do next.

Gas heating

Servicing. Checks and servicing by a Gas Safe registered engineer to keep your heating running safely.

Repairs and installs. Faults diagnosed and fixed, or new heating fitted when a repair no longer makes sense.

Plumbing

Repairs. Leaks, blockages and failed fittings put right, with quick service.

Fittings. Taps, pipework and water systems fitted neatly, tested and left tidy.

Energy performance certificates (EPC)

Assessment. An energy performance certificate (EPC) for a property you are selling or letting.

Clear rating. An A to G rating, with the steps that would improve it.
